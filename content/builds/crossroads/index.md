The Crossroads sits at the intersection of road and trail — drop bars for efficiency on pavement, clearance for light gravel, a geometry that works in both worlds without fully committing to either.

## The Hybrid Philosophy

Not every ride is a race. Not every road is paved. The Crossroads is for the commuter who sometimes takes the long way home through the park, the tourist who wants to explore without a dedicated touring setup.
