Emerald City Bouncer

2025-06-15

This thing is built specifically for city riding. It’s a very sporty handling quick curb popper. It is a rigid MTB turned into a city bouncer bike and it’s colored emerald green so, we are calling this one the Emerald City Bouncer.

  • This Hardrock Sport frame is made of Specialized butted CroMoly manufactured by Tange.
  • Looks like someone came along and touched up the paint with a pretty good color match but not the best handiwork.
  • Shiny high quality fenders match well in size with the plump but athletic tires.
  • Size is medium or medium large (19” seat tube), optimal fit for someone 5’2” to 5’10”.

The size of the tire for this build are the low end of what we like to use for crappy PNW roads, but that makes for sporty and quick rolling.

It has solid chromed fenders for transport during drizzle. That and the tires have some concept of siping going on means it can hold its own in the rain.

Build

The primary goal of this build was to make as simple a city bike as possible: a solid, cute build that no self-respecting bike thief would waste their time on. It could be a commuter with a rack or two but as is it is super fun, and will get you home if you get caught riding in the rain

(Note: cantilever brakes are sweet but not the best in the rain unless you keep ligthly applying the brakes constantly, squeegeeing the rim brake track for quicker stopping. So this is not a hardcore commuter.)

Frame

  • Specialized Hardrock Sport
  • Specialized DirectDrive Tubes by Tange.
  • Effective seatpost: 19”
  • Effective toptube: 21”

Goals

  • Killer commuter (read: fenders) in terms of performance
  • Cute look (nice color scheme and lots of chrome shiny parts)
  • Not a bike thief magnet: be of no value to serious bike thieves (of course, we cannot guarantee some methy tweaker will not do something stupid)

Restoration work

Full restoration serviced:

  • Fully regreased
    • Every bearing disassembled, cleaned, and rebuilt with fresh grease
    • Shifters got a full overhaul.
  • Shifters: fully overhauled. These Shimano friction ones last forever and the shifting is never de-indexed.
  • Classic 3x7 transmission
  • Swapped in a chrome handlebar with some rise and backsweep for comfort
  • Swapped in a chrome quil
  • Configured the thumbies to be under bar
  • Tires: Continential Town & Country are great city tires, fat (smooths out the bumps), shoulderless for quick steering, unbroken centerline (roll fast) with minor knobs (some minor off-road trackion, maybe anti-hydroplaning tech)
