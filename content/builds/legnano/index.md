Seems like it is a 1970, +/-2 years but probably a 1970.  Livery is
the iconic metallic gold/yellow paint finish (historically referred to
as "lizard yellow").

Vintage Italian that when through a phase as a fixie but restored it
to its original configuration, but zero fucks were given to finding
original parts. For example, modern two pivot rim brakes are much
better than old school single pivot.  Modern day manufactured skinny
700C rims and were able to squeeze 32mm tires in. Paselas are a nice ride.

So, great functional ride that can handle shitty Cascadian urban
asphault with the 32mm tires with solid rim brakes. Looks the
part. But no collector purist would be "fooled" but then this
bike wasn't built to hang on a wall. Very nice to ride.
