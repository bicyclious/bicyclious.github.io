We dialed this derelict punk styled beach cruiser for when you're
heading to the roller rink with storage for skates. Or grocery
shopping with the detachable basket.


## Build

- Nice comfy Oury grips
- A real bike saddle not a bench with springs
- Better pedals
- Nice crisp looking handlebar
- Basket (with handle) detaches with the click of a button
- Non-ridiculous tires for a plush but fast enough roll on asphault yet the tread could handle casual trails
- Streamers are 40 gallon garbage bags upcycled, punk style

## Cruisers are a 1930s classic design

Don't write off the cruiser as a serious bike! The original mountain bikes in the 1970s
where based on cruisers and balloon-tire paperboy bikes, like the Schwinn Excelsior.

[![Stan's 1934 Excelsior DX-2](https://images.theradavist.com/uploads/2026/05/Stans-1934-Excelsior-DX-2.jpg?w=1400&quality=75)](https://theradavist.com/1940-excelsior-klunker)


Even hardcore modern lycra adorned roadies
get their "brain rewired" riding a cruiser.
