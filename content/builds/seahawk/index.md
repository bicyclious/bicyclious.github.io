This build is named for the seahawk, the bird that hunts by dropping
straight out of the sky into the water for prizes, which is why the
ospray is also known as the fish hawk. This one was build to dive and
come back up, that is it floats via the big tires, foam tubing, and 
air tight bags (not pictured). And they we styled it a la the
colors of the Seattle Seahawks.

## The Build

The goal of this build was to make a bike that is made for jumping
into the water off docks. That means it has to be safe for the water
environment (no oil slicks) and relatively safe for the rider
(designed to have as few as possible sharp points (chainring guard,
handlebar with no pointy ends, etc.).

The chain is waxed as is the rest of the drive train. This way where
is no grease to create oil slicks when jumping this bike into the
water, like a seahawk.

- Beautiful matte purple Gary Fisher cromo frame (Fisher is one of the core OG mountain bike designers)
- DIY BMX style padding
- 3x7 reduced to 1x7, notice friction shifter on bottom of top tube 
- God Ring chain guard (keep those chainring cogs from gouging flesh upon crashes)
- 26" gray tires for the Seahawks color scheme
- Green Seahawks: handlebar tape, pedals, saddle
