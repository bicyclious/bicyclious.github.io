This build was kept mostly original because it is just so nice as
is. Everything was restored but undates were kept minimal.

The frame is made of high quality 575 cromo tubing. Nice stable long
wheel base. Front reach is long via that toptube. This bike is early
enough in the timeline that the manicly overfocused obsession with
short rearends had not taken over yet. The is a classic.

Bullmoose handlebars are lovely to look at and lovely to
ride, and a nod to the great Tom Ritchey who invented the bullmoose in
1980.

Beautiful sparkly maroon paint is in great shape (Raleigh was still a
proud apex beast of the bike world then so their paint jobs were
solide). Looks like someone did an OK job touching up the paint as
some point in the bike's history. Bonus: color coordinated jockey pulley wheel!
