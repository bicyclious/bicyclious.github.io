The cheap usual suspects:
- The larger type
  - $25 [Rockbro's knockoff of the One Up](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07PD9Y5D6?ref_=ppx_hzsearch_conn_dt_b_fed_asin_title_9&th=1)
    - $40 for [the real deal from One Up](https://www.oneupcomponents.com/products/comp-pedal)
    - $23 for [metal ones](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F37Y45HJ/ref=sspa_dk_detail_1) can look good. Nylons buck less when they scrape the ground.
- The smaller type
  - $23 [Fooker knockoffs of the classic Chester pedal](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07D66NDS3/ref=twister_B07L5RF5CW?_encoding=UTF8&th=1)
    - $60 for [real Chesters](https://www.raceface.com/products/chester-pedal)

The blinger options that we return to regularly
- ~$65 [MKS Gamma](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07R4Y2DZR?ref_=ppx_hzsearch_conn_dt_b_fed_asin_title_2&th=1)
- $60 [MKS Bear Traps](https://velo-orange.com/products/mks-xc-iii-bear-trap-pedal?variant=45023991005359)
  - $27 [SE knockoffs](https://www.amazon.com/SE-Bear-Trap-Pedals-Silver/dp/B0747TMP6V/ref=sr_1_24)


[Origin8 Pro-Grip II Pedal
Straps](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B007JWZ4MG?ref_=ppx_hzsearch_conn_dt_b_fed_asin_title_15&th=1)
makes the best pedal straps for ~$34. Putting straps on your feet is a
bit of commitment but not as commited and old-school toe straps in the
bear cages of old. No thanks to that. But these straps are excellent
for pulling up the rear of a loaded bike to get over curbs and such.
