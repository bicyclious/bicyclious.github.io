Bubblicious got its name from the color scheme — that particular bubblegum pink that shouldn't work but absolutely does. A full-suspension trail bike that rides as fun as it looks.

## Trail Tested

Documented at Lake Crescent on the Olympic Peninsula. The bike looks like dessert but handles like a tool. Low center of gravity, plush rear suspension, grips the trail through loose-over-hardpack.

## Style Notes

The outfit coordination in the photos was intentional. When the bike and rider match, the whole thing becomes a statement. Bubblicious is that statement.
