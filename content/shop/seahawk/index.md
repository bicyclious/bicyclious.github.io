Named for the bird that hunts by dropping straight out of the sky. The
ospray a.k.a. the fish hawk. This one was build to dive and come back up.

The chain is waxed as is the rest of the drive train. There is no grease
to create oil slicks when jumping this bike into the water, like a seahawk.

## The Build

Cromoly steel frame with a classic geometry that puts you over the
front wheel where you want to be when the trail drops away. Flat bars
keep the handling honest. 26-inch wheels roll over roots and rocks the
way they were always meant to.

## Riding Notes

This bike rewards riders who commit. Brake late, stay loose, trust the
steel. The Seahawk doesn't ask much — just point it downhill and hold
on.
