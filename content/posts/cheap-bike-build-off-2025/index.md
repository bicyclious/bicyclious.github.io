The Cheap Bike Build-Off has become an annual event. For 2025, we built
the Bad Banana, named after the children's book, Pete the Cat and the Bad Banana.

![](pete_the_cat_book_cover.jpeg)


## The Build-Off

The Build-Off is a knockout tournament of cheap bike builds.
Globally the budget was $150 and you has about a month to do the build.
Post your build and then the knockout brackets of sudden death.
