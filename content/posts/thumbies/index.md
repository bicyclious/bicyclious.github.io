Trigger shifters are nice enough, but we love us some thumbies at
Bicyclious. They were the original shifters for mountain bikes. They
are scrumptious!

Thumbies are simple and effective, durable for
decades, unlike trigger shifters which gum up over the years so
degrade annoyingly. They're a pain in the ass to restore. To do so
well requires an ultrasonic cleaner (which we do have) meaning
they do not qualify as delicious. Restoring thumbies is a simple
overhaul that requires no special tools nor machines.

## Friction thumbies

- All the control smarts are in the rider's brain.
- Analogous to a manual transmission automobile
- Some OG thumbies have a switch for going between indexed and friction, good for failover if indexing gets wonky on a ride and out without tools

## Modern thumbies

Commercially, new thumbies are available. Older one have limited shifting range, maxing out at eight.
