# Product Requirement Document (PRD): Bicyclious Combo Logo Specification

## 1. Overview & Brand Alignment
* **Brand Name:** "Bicyclious" (Spelled exactly: Capital B, lowercase i-c-y-c-l-i-o-u-s).
* **Core Product:** A professional commercial logo for an independent bicycle company focusing on custom builds and mechanical craftsmanship.
* **Design Vision:** A polished, mid-1980s NYC street-style graffiti blackbook masterpiece adapted into a clean commercial logo. The design must masterfully merge high-vibrancy organic aesthetics with raw mechanical components.
* **Key Visual Anchor:** The logo combines a prominent standalone icon (the letter "B" nestled within a large gear) with a tightly clustered, hyper-stylized foreground wordmark, all structurally framed by an integrated bicycle drivetrain assembly.

## 2. Composition & Spatial Layout
* **The Structural Drivetrain Frame:** The background consists of a side-profile bicycle gear and chain assembly floating in empty space, rendered as a single continuous horizontal loop.
    * **Left Focal Point:** One large circular metal chainring (cog).
    * **Right Focal Point:** One significantly smaller circular metal cog. Both cogs' center points are horizontally aligned.
    * **The Chain Path:** A highly stylized bicycle chain forms the definitive, un-warped outer border of the entire composition. It wraps securely around the outer perimeter of both gears in a single continuous loop. The top and bottom lengths of the chain run perfectly straight and horizontal between the cogs, creating a clean clearance gap that never physically touches or contours into the letters.
* **The Standalone Icons & Alignment:**
    * **The Initial "B":** The uppercase letter "B" is massive (significantly enlarged to nearly fill the entire inner diameter of the large left chainring cog). It sits centered directly inside the cog so that the "B-plus-cog" unit functions perfectly as an independent, standalone brand mark.
    * **The Final "s":** The lowercase "s" is concentrically aligned dead-center inside the right, smaller circular cog, balancing the horizontal drivetrain layout perfectly.
* **Foreground Wordmark Spacing:** The remaining letters ("icycliou") sit in the foreground between the two cogs. The typography consists of ultra-fat, hyper-inflated balloon forms with extremely tight, crowded letter spacing and zero dead space between them.
* **Negative Space Treatment:** The interior background area enclosed by the chain is a solid, deep black. This negative space contains subtle, integrated pink highlights reflecting off the backs of the glossy foreground glyphs.

## 3. Detailed Style & Rendering Guidelines

For graffiti shines, the e light source is placed in the top-left corner.

### 3.1 Foreground Typography ("Bicyclious")
* **Aesthetic Era:** Authentic mid-1980s NYC aerosol graffiti bubble lettering. Avoid modern vector graffiti, clean corporate branding, minimalist geometry, or esports/gaming styles.
* **Form & Spacing:** Letters are plump, overinflated, fat balloon-like forms. Spacing must be extremely tight and crowded, making the characters look as if they are actively overinflating and pushing against each other inside the containment of the outer chain border.
* **Specific Glyph Requirements:**
    * **The "l" Glyph:** Must be rendered straight up and down, avoiding a long horizontal base of traditional capital "L" characteristics.
    * **The Final "s":** Must be compact, balanced, and perfectly contained, completely free of exaggerated tails, sweeps, or decorative flourishes to maximize commercial readability.
* **Color & Texture:** Rendered with a glossy, airbrushed bubblegum texture. The lettering features a strict, uniform vertical gradient transitioning from the signature brand pink (**#D70969**) at the base to a soft, white-ish pink at the crest. All letters share this identical color mapping to ensure absolute visual consistency.
* **Lighting:** Accented with classic 1980s-style sharp white specular highlights to provide authentic 3D depth and an overinflated, reflective sheen.

### 3.2 Background Framing Elements (Cogs & Chain)
* **Interpretive Realism:** The assembly utilizes a stylized, "comic book" concept of a bicycle drivetrain rather than a micro-accurate mechanical reproduction.
* **Scale & Links:** To match the bold weight of the graffiti typography, the individual chain links are oversized, fat, and heavy. The entire loop should comprise approximately 40 distinct links (in contrast to a real bike chain's 100+ links), with each link clearly defined and physically engaging the teeth of both cogs.
* **Material & Texture:** hihly shiny polished chrome metal with graffii shines.
* **Graffiti Chrome-Piece Effects:** Fully loaded with classic street-art rendering techniques, including high-contrast metallic reflections, deep reflection horizontal split-lines, specular highlights, clean dark outlines, and vibrant starburst glints.
* **Depth:** Anchored by light gray dimensional drop shadows to visually separate the chrome mechanical frame from the deep black background fill.

## 4. Design Exclusions & Constraints (Do Not Include)
* **Typography Violations:** No modern vector fonts, corporate tech branding, esports-style vector sharp edges, comic-book pop art (Ben-Day dots), or generic street-art murals.
* **Structural Excess:** Do not include multiple chains, more than two cogs, or extra unrelated bicycle components (such as pedals, spokes, or hubs).
* **Decorative Fluffs:** Avoid stickers, background clutter, external splatters, or long flourishing script tails on the final "s".
* **Composition Rules:** Do not allow the top and bottom exterior straight runs of the chain to warp, sag, or contour tightly into individual letters; the loop must remain a clean, simple, un-warped enclosure spanning the two cogs.

It wraps securely around the outer perimeter of both gears in a single continuous loop. The top and bottom lengths of the chain run perfectly straight and horizontal between the cogs, creating a clean clearance gap that never physically touches or contours into the letters.
